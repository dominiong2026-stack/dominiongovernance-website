# Dominion In-House Search and AI Discovery Programme
Status: Proposed operating standard — pending owner approval and deployment verification
Date: 2026-11-21

## Objectives
Increase qualified inbound enquiries for executive advisory, business transformation, AI governance, HR advisory and executive coaching. No promise of Google rankings or ChatGPT citations.

## Controls
1. No auto-publishing. Human owner approves every public article and change.
2. No client names, project coordinates, confidential materials, or identifiable engagement details without written clearance.
3. Verify standards, legal claims and professional credentials against primary sources.
4. Distinguish advisory services from regulated legal, certification, statutory audit and cybersecurity services.
5. Use canonical URLs and descriptive page titles; never manufacture city-specific pages or testimonials.
6. Review every link, factual assertion, accessibility issue, and CTA before publication.
7. Record author, reviewer, review date, sources and change history.

## Content pipeline
Research topic and intent -> map to existing page -> outline -> source verification -> editorial review -> preview build -> accessibility and link QA -> approval -> production release -> monitor.

## Initial content queue
1. Board oversight of AI: questions directors should ask.
2. Executive advisory: signals an organisation needs independent intervention.
3. Leadership transition: preparing managers for greater responsibility.
4. ISO/IEC 42001 readiness: governance questions before certification engagement.

## Priority technical checks
- Canonical host consistency, HTTP redirects, indexability and robots rules.
- All sitemap entries resolve successfully and have the intended canonical.
- Structured data matches visible content; avoid unsupported ratings, reviews, FAQ rich-result claims.
- Page titles, descriptions, heading hierarchy, Open Graph and image alt text.
- Core Web Vitals on mobile; image dimensions and lazy loading.
- Forms, privacy disclosures and lead routing.
- Search Console ownership, sitemap submission, indexing and query baseline.
- Analytics with consent and privacy controls.

## Measurement
Weekly: search impressions, organic clicks, CTR, indexed pages, branded/nonbranded queries, top landing pages, form submissions and qualified leads.
Monthly: content published, ranking changes, lead quality, conversion rates, and attributable engagements.
Do not report rankings, revenue or ROI without measurement evidence.

## Release gates
- Repository branch reviewed.
- Internal links verified.
- Production host identified and deployment credentials validated without exposing secrets.
- Preview QA passed.
- Owner approves promotion to production.
