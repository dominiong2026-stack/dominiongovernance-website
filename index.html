The Dominion Model

Enterprise capability. Direct senior accountability.

Complex organisations do not always need more consulting layers. They need the right senior people, close to the problem and accountable for the outcome.

01 Senior-led throughout

Direct access to accountable executive judgement from diagnosis through implementation.

02 Tailored specialist capability

The right disciplines are assembled around the condition rather than forcing a standard consulting package.

03 Faster mobilisation

Shorter decision paths allow the firm to establish the position and organise action without avoidable delay.

04 Focused investment

Client investment is concentrated on senior expertise, implementation and measurable value—not institutional layers.
